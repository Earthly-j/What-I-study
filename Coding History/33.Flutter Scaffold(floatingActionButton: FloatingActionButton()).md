```

  setState(() => _count++);

```

floatingActionButtonAnimator:
-----------------------------

![captionless image](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1400/format:webp/1*OCuRhsk7LCajdv5snQiSAw.gif)

The `scaling` animator dictates that during the transition from one `floatingActionButtonLocation` to another the FAB will:

1.  **Scale Down:** The FAB briefly **shrinks** (scales down to zero or a very small size) as it leaves its original position.
2.  **Move (Offset):** It simultaneously moves horizontally and vertically to its new position.
3.  **Scale Up:** The FAB **grows** (scales back up to its normal size) as it arrives at its new destination.
