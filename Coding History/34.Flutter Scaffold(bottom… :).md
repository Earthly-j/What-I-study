*   `BottomNavigationBar` **Required Item property:** the `BottomNavigationBar` was seen as a bar composed of small, clickable **items**(`BottomNavigationBarItem`). The focus was on the individual button element.
*   `NavigationBar` **Required Destination:** the `NavigationBar` emphasizes the concept of **Navigation**. Each tab represents a whole **destination**—a top-level view or screen the user can navigate to. The change signals a stricter adherence to design principles where the bottom bar is fundamentally about routing the user to a primary location.
