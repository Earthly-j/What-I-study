What's Meta ?
-------------

The term “Meta” in the context of the Emacs key, or the **M-** prefix (like `M-x`), draws its meaning from the Greek prefix **meta-** , which means:

*   **Beyond**
*   **After**
*   **About (its own category)**

In computing and programming, “meta” is used to signify a higher-level or self-referential concept.
