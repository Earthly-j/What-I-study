Hello there! 👋 Today, I’m going to tell you all about the `**Stack**` **class** in Flutter. When you need that _depth_—that layered, overlapping effect that makes modern UIs pop—you need something more powerful. You need the `Stack`.

The `**Stack**` widget orders its children sequentially, determining which widget is visible on top:

*   The **first child widget** added to the `Stack` is placed at the **bottom** layer.
*   Each subsequent child widget is placed **on top** of the previous ones. The **newest child widget** is always on the **top** layer.

5. fit
-------

**fit** property shows “How to define size non-**Positioned** child widgets of **IndexedStack**”. Its default value is **StackFit.loose**.

### 1. `StackFit.loose` (Default)

**Behavior:** The `Stack` loosens its constraints before passing them to the children.

*   **Constraints Passed:** The children receive the Stack’s maximum constraints but are free to choose **any size smaller**than that maximum.

### 2. `StackFit.expand`

**Behavior:** The `Stack` forces its children to take the maximum available size.

*   **Constraints Passed:** The children receive **tight (rigid) constraints** equal to the Stack’s final, resolved size (minus any margins/padding).

### 3. `StackFit.passthrough`

**Behavior:** The `Stack` does **no modification** to its incoming constraints; it passes them _directly_ to its children.

*   **Constraints Passed:** The children receive the **exact same constraints** (loose or tight) that the `Stack` received from its parent widget.

6. `Stack.clipBehavior`
------------------------

### 6.1. `Clip.none`

**Behavior:** The `Stack` performs **no clipping**.

### 6.2. `Clip.hardEdge` (Default)

**Behavior:** The `Stack` uses a quick, direct method to **cut off** any content attempting to draw outside its bounds

### 6.3. `Clip.antiAlias`

**Behavior:** The `Stack` uses a clipping method that incorporates **anti-aliasing**.

### 6.4. `Clip.antiAliasWithSaveLayer`

**Behavior:** The `Stack` uses anti-aliasing and a resource-intensive `**SaveLayer**` operation on the canvas.
